THE PROJECT

Apex Motors needed an electrifying launch film for their flagship grand tourer. Unlike combustion engines that roar with exhaust acoustics, electric vehicles present a distinct sound challenge: they are silent. The goal was to build acoustic gravitas through synthesized power, torque curves, and optical rhythm.

MY ROLE

Commercial Video Editing Speed Ramping & Match Cuts Sound Design & Foley Night Anamorphic Color Grade
CREATIVE DIRECTION

THE APPROACH

Velocity measured in micro-moments. Traditional car commercials rely on wide track shots with drone pans. We chose macro proximity: the tire groove slicing through rainwater at 120 FPS, the instantaneous iris dilation of the driver, and reflections of Tokyo neon racing across carbon fiber bodywork.

We implemented a whip-velocity match-cut system: entering a frame at 600% speed, decelerating to a 100% dead-stop on a single bead of moisture, and detonating into a 240 FPS slow-motion corner drift on the exact downbeat of a sub-bass kick.

The color palette was anchored in nocturnal cyan-teal reflections balanced against tungsten streetlights and rich specular highlights on the matte obsidian finish.
