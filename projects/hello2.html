THE PROJECT

HELLO 2 explores fluid motion design integrated with rhythmic editing. The piece focuses on clean typography, seamless spatial shifts, and audio-reactive pacing.

MY ROLE

Motion Design Video Editing Typographic Direction Sound Synchronization
CREATIVE DIRECTION

THE APPROACH

Motion rooted in musicality. Every graphic reveal was mapped to frequency peaks in the audio stems. Transitions move through camera whip-pans and whip-zooms that preserve directional momentum across cuts.

We implemented a minimal, Swiss-inspired typographic grid to keep complex motion feeling structured rather than cluttered.

The final cut achieves a seamless loop, engineered for high replayability and social retention.
