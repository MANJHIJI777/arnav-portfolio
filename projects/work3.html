THE APPROACH

Managing the rise and fall of visual tempo. Rather than maintaining uniform intensity, this edit breathes: high-kinetic passages give way to contemplative wide shots, creating dynamic range that prevents viewer fatigue.

Motion elements and typographic layouts were keyed directly into 3D scene camera trackers, giving title cards authentic physical presence within the frame.

Audio stems were split into dialogue, Foley textures, and low-frequency synthesis to deliver a wide spatial soundscape that fills the stereo field.
