THE PROJECT

Axiom Systems introduced Unit-1, a desktop neural hub with spatial projection. The film had to demonstrate how floating volumetric UI feels completely tangible, tactile, and mathematically grounded—avoiding cheesy sci-fi holograms in favor of industrial precision.

MY ROLE

3D Motion Direction UI Choreography Haptic Sound Synthesis Editorial Pacing
CREATIVE DIRECTION

THE APPROACH

Motion rooted in physical inertia. Floating digital UI often feels weightless and disconnected. We engineered a physics model where volumetric nodes possess mass, spring damping, and momentum. When a user turns the knurled dial, UI rays flex like tension cables before snapping to their grid coordinates.

Every optical transition was designed with frame-level precision in After Effects and Cinema 4D. Wireframe nodes expand along exact isometric vectors, illuminated by laser-sharp light caustics across the anodized aluminum chassis.

To make light feel tactile, each holographic interaction was paired with custom synthesized glass and stone clicks: high-frequency 4kHz transients with rapid 15ms decays that communicate instant physical response.
