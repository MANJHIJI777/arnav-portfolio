THE PROJECT

I75 CPA Review is a specialized test preparation platform. Most educational videos in this sector are dry, slide-heavy, and uninspiring. The mandate was to treat the CPA exam not as corporate compliance, but as a grueling, cinematic gauntlet—reframing the candidate's journey from anxiety into mastery.

MY ROLE

Video Editing Motion Graphics Sound Design Color Grading Storytelling

METRICS ACHIEVED

84.2% average watch duration on YouTube Pre-roll; 3.2× conversion spike on the course enrollment landing page.

CREATIVE DIRECTION

THE APPROACH

Pacing dictated by psychological tension. The cut begins in an agonizingly quiet rhythm: solitary keyclicks, muffled fluorescent room tone, and tight close-ups of an exhausted candidate facing a 4-hour countdown clock. By establishing silence first, every subsequent sound element hit with amplified force.

At the 0:24 mark, the edit shifts violently from passive observation into kinetic acceleration. We accelerated the cut frequency from 3.2-second holds down to 8-frame rhythmic bursts, synchronizing financial ledger diagrams, glowing bar charts, and passing skyscrapers with rising sub-bass impacts.

Rather than generic corporate voiceovers, the voice track was edited down to razor-sharp declarations. Every pause was scrubbed of hesitation. Motion graphics were restrained to architectural HUD overlays rather than flashy cartoons, ensuring the tone maintained absolute authority.
