THE PROJECT

FOURTH is a high-octane commercial campaign exploring visual acceleration, heavy sound design sync, and modern typographic titling for brand storytelling.

MY ROLE

Commercial Video Editing Speed Ramping Motion Design Sound Design Color Grading
CREATIVE DIRECTION

THE APPROACH

Rhythm governs every cut. We structured the timeline around an escalating BPM curve, starting at a deliberate 90 BPM pulse before accelerating into a barrage of synchronized visual impacts.

Kinetic typography was integrated directly into the physical environment using planar tracking, letting titles sit with weight and depth behind foreground elements.

Color timing utilized an expressive high-contrast split-tone LUT curve, bringing out deep blacks and vivid specular reflections on metallic surfaces.
